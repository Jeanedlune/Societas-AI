# Task-060 - Siapkan Release MVP Lokal

- **Phase:** 6 - Validasi dan rilis
- **Priority:** P0
- **Depends on:** 013, 050, 051, 052, 053, 054, 055, 056, 058, 059
- **Source:** [Doc 09](../docs/09-security-sandbox-observability.md), [Doc 10](../docs/10-contracts-mvp-roadmap.md), [README](../README.md)

## Goal
Kemas MVP supaya pengguna dapat setup dan menjalankan workflow general secara lokal.

**Baseline:** [deployment lokal MVP](../docs/00-mvp-implementation-baseline.md).

## Scope
Siapkan packaging/build, first-run config, health diagnostics, sample workspace, release notes, and support checklist.

## Acceptance criteria
- Fresh install/start/stop berhasil mengikuti dokumentasi.
- Sample workspace mendemonstrasikan strategi konten dari tren dan sales data sintetis.
- Contract, unit, integration, security, dan end-to-end tests lulus.
- Known limitations, provider setup, data handling, dan recovery documented.
- Approval tetap wajib sebelum aksi eksternal.

## Handoff
Jangan menyebut fitur yang belum tersedia sebagai implemented.
