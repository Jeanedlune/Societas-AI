# Task-042 - Implementasikan Content Drafting Workflow

- **Phase:** 4 - Workflow general
- **Priority:** P0
- **Depends on:** 038, 039, 040, 041
- **Source:** [Doc 01](../docs/01-vision-and-core.md), [Doc 05](../docs/05-artifacts-memory-tools.md), [Doc 10](../docs/10-contracts-mvp-roadmap.md)

## Goal
Content Creator menghasilkan draft sesuai audience, channel, brief, brand voice, dan evidence.

## Scope
Implementasikan draft inputs, claim/source linkage, output formats, revision cycles, and artifact versions.

## Acceptance criteria
- Draft terhubung ke research/sales artifact versions yang dipakai.
- Unsupported claim ditandai atau dikeluarkan.
- Channel/audience/length constraints dipenuhi.
- Revision membuat versi baru tanpa overwrite sebelumnya.
- Tests mencakup source missing, constraints, and content versioning.

## Handoff
Draft belum dipublikasikan hanya karena task selesai.
